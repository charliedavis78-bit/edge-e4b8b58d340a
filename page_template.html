New to betting? How to read this

Odds like -170 or +136. A minus number is the favorite: -170 means bet $170 to win $100 profit. A plus number is the underdog: +136 means bet $100 to win $136 profit.

Payout vs profit. Payout is what you get back, including your own stake. Profit is the part on top.

Market vs model. The market chance is what the odds imply. The model chance is this tool's own estimate. A lean only shows when the model sees a clearly better chance than the odds pay for.

Most days the right answer is no bet. Even a good lean loses often. Start small, only use money you can afford to lose, and check who is injured before you bet.
